村野 藤吾
Murano Togo
Architect
Life1891–1984 ／ FromSaga Prefecture

Biography
Born in Karatsu, Saga Prefecture. He graduated from the Department of Architecture, Faculty of Science and Engineering, Waseda University, in 1918, and in the same year joined the Watanabe Setsu architectural office in Osaka, where he gained practical experience. In 1929 he left the office and established the Murano Architectural Office, which he renamed Murano & Mori Architectural Office in 1949. He worked on many buildings, including the World Peace Memorial Cathedral (Hiroshima City; completed 1954), the Ube City Watanabe-Okina Memorial Hall (Ube, Yamaguchi Prefecture; completed 1937), and the Nihonbashi Takashimaya store (Chuo Ward, Tokyo; originally designed by Takahashi Teitaro and completed in 1933, with Murano responsible for extensions from 1952 onward). He received the Japan Art Academy Prize in 1953 and the Order of Culture in 1967, and served as a member of the Japan Art Academy. In 1973 he received an honorary doctorate from his alma mater, Waseda University. He died in 1984 at the age of 93.
Legacy
An architect who, over a career spanning more than half a century, worked across religious architecture, public facilities, and department store extensions. At Nihonbashi Takashimaya, he took on the task of extending a building originally designed by another architect, leaving his mark on a building that remains in business today.
Selected Works
World Peace Memorial Cathedral (Hiroshima, Hiroshima Prefecture; completed 1954; extant; Important Cultural Property, designated 2006), Ube City Watanabe-Okina Memorial Hall (Ube, Yamaguchi Prefecture; completed 1937; extant; Important Cultural Property, designated 2005), Nihonbashi Takashimaya (Chuo Ward, Tokyo; completed 1933, with Murano responsible for extensions from 1952 onward; extant; Important Cultural Property, designated 2009)
Collections
3 buildings designated as Important Cultural Properties (designation years as listed under Selected Works). A dedicated memorial museum or archive has not been confirmed.
