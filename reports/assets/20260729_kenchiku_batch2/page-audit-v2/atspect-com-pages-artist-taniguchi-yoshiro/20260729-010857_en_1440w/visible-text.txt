谷口 吉郎
Taniguchi Yoshiro
Architect
Life1904–1979 ／ FromIshikawa Prefecture
Link to this artist's page
Copy link
QR code
Biography
Born in Kanazawa, Ishikawa Prefecture. After attending the Fourth Higher School, he entered the Department of Architecture, Faculty of Engineering, Tokyo Imperial University, in 1925 and graduated in 1928; for his graduation research he was guided by Ito Chuta. He then proceeded to the graduate school of Tokyo Imperial University, where he studied under Sano Toshikata. In 1930 he became a lecturer at Tokyo Institute of Technology and, through the ranks of associate professor and professor, taught there until his retirement in 1965. In 1964 he became the founding director of the Meiji-mura open-air museum and, after its opening in 1965, continued to serve as director until 1979. In 1967 he established his own design office. He designed buildings including the Komoro Municipal Toson Memorial Museum (Komoro, Nagano Prefecture; completed 1957), the Toyokan Gallery of the Tokyo National Museum (Taito Ward, Tokyo; completed 1968), and the main building of the National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (Chiyoda Ward, Tokyo; completed 1969). He received the Architectural Institute of Japan Academic Prize in 1942, its Prize for Design in 1949, and the Japan Art Academy Prize in 1961, and in 1973 was named a Person of Cultural Merit and received the Order of Culture. He died in 1979 at the age of 74.
Legacy
An architect who designed numerous cultural facilities, including museums and memorial halls. In addition to his long teaching career at Tokyo Institute of Technology, he served as the founding director of the Meiji-mura open-air museum, where he was also involved in relocating and preserving historic buildings from across Japan.
Selected Works
Komoro Municipal Toson Memorial Museum (Komoro, Nagano Prefecture; completed 1957, opened to the public 1958; extant; nationally Registered Tangible Cultural Property, registered 2025), Toyokan Gallery of the Tokyo National Museum (Taito Ward, Tokyo; completed 1968; extant; no national cultural property designation or registration confirmed), main building of the National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (Chiyoda Ward, Tokyo; completed 1969; extant; no national cultural property designation or registration confirmed)
Collections
1 nationally Registered Tangible Cultural Property (Komoro Municipal Toson Memorial Museum, registered 2025). No cultural property designation or registration has been confirmed for the Toyokan Gallery of the Tokyo National Museum or the main building of the National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo. A dedicated memorial museum or archive has not been confirmed.
